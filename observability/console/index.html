Get refused by the guest list

console straight to httpbin, with POST

Every request should come back 403. httpbin's proxy refuses them because console is not in the AuthorizationPolicy, and the httpbin app never sees them.

Make httpbin fail

console to the front door, then httpbin, which answers 500 on purpose

This is what a broken service looks like from outside: a 5xx spike in Grafana and a red line from the front door to httpbin in Kiali.

Make httpbin slow

console to the front door, then httpbin, which waits 2 seconds before answering

Everything succeeds, but httpbin's p95 latency climbs to about 2 seconds. Requests go 10 at a time, so 20 take about 4 seconds.

Grafana and Kiali trail your click by 10 to 20 seconds: Prometheus collects the proxies' counters every 5 seconds, and the charts average over the last 30. The links above work while bash scripts/dashboards.sh is running.
